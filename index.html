Functional listening screening

These original prompts are symptom-domain aids, not standardized or validated questionnaires. They are not CHAPPS, Fisher's checklist, SIFTER or a validated adult scale. Do not apply a diagnostic cut-off; use authorized standardized tools according to their manuals when appropriate.

Standard screening tool selection

Select the authorised instrument that fits the person's age, referral question, language and available norms. Age-based guidance is a prompt for clinician choice, not an automatic diagnosis or a substitute for each tool's manual.

Enter age in Patient & history, then select an authorised tool. No universally accepted screening method applies to every adult.

The app does not reproduce copyrighted or author-controlled questionnaire items. Obtain and administer the official version, in its authorised language, and record its official result below. Do not translate or score a standard tool yourself and present it as validated.

Indian-developed screening resources

SCAP (children): Indian-developed 12-item screening checklist. Published research reports a cut score of 6 in the studied school-age sample; use only with the authorised checklist and its instructions. This app does not reproduce its items.

Open SCAP research record / accessible paper · Indian practice survey and screening context

SCAP-A (adults): The published development study involved older adults aged 55–75 years and a separate family checklist. Do not assume validation for all adults aged 18+. The paper's preliminary findings note that further validation was needed.

Open SCAP-A article and PDF · Open later SCAP-A study

STAP: Indian-developed performance-based screening test for school-aged children, not a symptom questionnaire. It requires appropriate test materials and administration; do not simulate it with the checklist items in this app.

STAP preliminary report · STAP validation study

The papers are research sources, not proof of permission to republish test items. The app links to the sources and records clinician-entered results; it does not copy author-controlled item wording or automatically calculate a standardized score.
Buffalo Model Questionnaire–Revised (BMQ-R)

The BMQ-R is a 48-characteristic screening questionnaire completed by the patient, parent or teacher. Use the official form and manual linked below; this app does not reproduce the copyrighted item text. The form and manual are free to download and use in practice; the official interactive scoring tool is an EAA member benefit.

Open official BMQ-R form (PDF) · Open administration and interpretation manual (PDF) · Official EAA information page

Important: BMQ-R is not India-specific and is not a diagnostic test. Use the manual, consider age, grade, respondent, language comprehension and prior therapies, and interpret alongside history and formal audiological testing. Its published manual describes age bands of under 6, 6–18, and over 18 years. Do not treat this as validated in Hindi, Punjabi or other Indian languages unless a validated version is available.

Central auditory test results

Record only tests administered. Enter the correct age/language normative reference and verify scoring direction for each test. Z-score flags are review prompts, not universal diagnostic criteria.

The prototype flags entered Z-scores at or below −2 SD and −3 SD for review only. CAPD diagnosis depends on test-specific norms, reliability, pattern across measures, functional complaints, peripheral hearing and clinician judgement.
