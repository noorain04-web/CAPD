Standard screening tool selection

This India-focused prototype offers SCAP and an app-adapted BMQ-R draft in the child pathway, and SCAP-A in the adult pathway. Verify age and language suitability and use authorised versions/instructions for standardized administration. No checklist diagnoses CAPD.

Enter age in Patient & history, then select an authorised tool. No universally accepted screening method applies to every adult.

Use authorised SCAP, BMQ-R or SCAP-A forms and their official instructions for standardized administration. The embedded BMQ-R draft and the app's Hindi/Punjabi prompts are not verified verbatim or validated versions of the official instruments.

Central auditory test results

Record only tests administered. Enter the correct age/language normative reference and verify scoring direction for each test. Z-score flags are review prompts, not universal diagnostic criteria.

The prototype flags entered Z-scores at or below −2 SD and −3 SD for review only. CAPD diagnosis depends on test-specific norms, reliability, pattern across measures, functional complaints, peripheral hearing and clinician judgement.
